## Corrections to the motivating synthesis

1. **Growth is established prior territory.** DEN, CURL, and online mixtures of tasks already address capacity expansion and/or latent task structure. A fixed expert set in the 1998 Wolpert-Kawato paper is not evidence that subsequent research left growth unexplored.
2. **The 179.8-degree number has a precise object.** The rerun yields 179.807 degrees for the two columns of the *mean finite write-effect matrix*. It is neither an angle between gradients nor the mean of per-seed angles. The paper reports the quantity it computed.
3. **Conflict does not imply a necessary split.** Irreducible noise, an unsuitable metric, a missing feature, or optimization constraints can generate conflict. Even a world with several contexts may be represented by one recurrent model.
4. **Before and after responsibility differ.** An outcome may legitimately assign responsibility for learning once observed; it cannot choose the prediction that is already being evaluated. The paper does not allege that the 1998 model itself violates this boundary.
5. **Copies are not free and historical success has a scope.** The substrate diagnostic uses full internal copies as an instrument. The proposed learner must pay for copies and determine which conditional capabilities remain applicable.
6. **A high relative weight is not adequacy.** The softmax can confidently select the least bad member of an entirely wrong model family.

## Statistical conventions

The timing controls use 64 independently seeded streams per condition, not 64 individual observations. Each stream contains 20,000 trials; its first is excluded from scoring. The confidence intervals use Student-t quantiles over stream means. The two hazard conditions use the same seed list. All per-seed values are retained. No fitted benchmark hyperparameters or growing modules are present.

The cross-write diagnostic uses the upstream default of twelve initializations. It measures a narrowly configured 16 x 16 substrate and preserves the original oracle balancing procedure. No p-value, universal representational limit, or broad biological claim is inferred from its means.

The prospective protocol was written after the diagnostic analysis. It has not been run or externally preregistered. Future results must not be described as belonging to the present executed study.
